The High Garden Story

A terrace built for
every kind of evening.

Eight floors above SB Road, High Garden opens onto uninterrupted sky — string-lit greenery, a skyline backdrop, and a room that feels like it was designed for the evening you're planning, whichever one that is.

The kitchen refuses to specialise in just one cuisine. North Indian kebabs share the pass with wood-fired pizza, Chinese wok dishes and Continental plates, while the bar keeps a serious cocktail list, curated gins and beer on tap running late into the night.

Owner Abhishek is usually somewhere on the floor — picking the night's playlist, checking on a birthday table, making sure a private event goes exactly to plan.
